Case studies

Five projects, with the lessons left in.

Ridecell first, then Prezent AI.

✶Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

People Operations built from zero

Challenge

On day one there was no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and employee questions had no tracked channel.

What I did

Built a 20+ procedure SOP library with named owners, backup coverage, and annual reviews. Launched ASKPeople on Jira and Confluence with tiered SLAs. Made Sapling the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier connections.

Result

Coverage grew from 3 countries to 5, with 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits. Approval cycles dropped from 1 to 2 days to 2 to 3 hours, and 5 to 10 tickets a day were resolved in 24 to 48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

▲Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

One approval path for every HRIS change

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process that created audit risk. Early pushback said a form would be too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form that requires documentation and approval before any HRIS change, plus Notion workflows for New Hire and Offboarding. Brought all stakeholders in and held firm.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed before, and preboarding triggered within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

ClaudeNotionRipplingSlack
◆Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

A reorganization with cost visibility

Challenge

A reorganization affecting 18 employees across the US and India needed accurate severance and COBRA numbers, fast.

What I did

Built AI-enabled severance and cost modeling with Claude and Gemini that calculated severance, COBRA cost, and COBRA months owed, and drafted the agreements. Tracked every step on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

Leadership saw reorganization cost for the first time, and agreement prep fell from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

30-60-90

A proposed plan built around the success picture in the role description.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet HRBPs, Recruiting, Finance, Legal, IT, and Payroll to learn where support breaks down today.
  • Map the employee and candidate lifecycle, including who owns each step and where Greenhouse hands off.
  • Meet the People Operations and Talent Acquisition Operations teams one on one.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Run a focused audit, like the 60-day review I ran at Prezent AI, of data quality, documentation, and controls.
  • Draft ownership, service standards, and escalation paths with the Director, People Programs, Systems and Analytics.
  • Pilot one change with a documented approval path, the pattern behind the Employee Action Form.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Publish SOPs and self-service resources for the highest-volume lifecycle processes.
  • Set a monthly planning rhythm for prioritization, dependencies, risks, and decisions.
  • Agree on operating metrics and service levels, and shortlist Greenhouse workflow improvements.
By month 6

The outcome

  • People Operations and Talent Acquisition Operations have clear ownership, service standards, escalation paths, and operating rhythms.
  • Core lifecycle processes are documented, measurable, and supported by controls and self-service.
  • One integrated roadmap with managed dependencies, trusted by leaders and partners.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with the Senior Director, People Operations, and the partners across the People team.
